# Meridian IP and Commercialization Brief

**Date:** August 26, 2026

## Executive conclusion

Meridian has credible commercial and intellectual-property potential, but its value will come from an implemented, trusted product rather than ownership of a broad idea. The recommended path is a premium household subscription, validated through concierge onboarding, followed by advisor, employer, and white-label distribution.

## Initial market position

Meridian should serve financially complex households with multiple accounts, shared obligations, irregular income, scattered bills and receipts, and a need for clarity without manual budgeting. Its promise is: one trustworthy picture of what happened, what is coming, why the system believes it, and what needs attention.

## Revenue model

- Meridian Individual: approximately $15-$20 per month.
- Meridian Household: approximately $30-$45 per month.
- Use a time-limited trial or money-back guarantee instead of a permanent feature-rich free tier.
- Do not sell financial data, use advertising, or accept paid placement inside recommendations.

## Go-to-market sequence

1. Recruit 10 design-partner households for founder-led onboarding.
2. Prove document-to-transaction reconciliation, Commitment discovery, funding forecasts, and explainable review.
3. Convert the strongest five households to paid use.
4. Expand to 25-50 founding households through referrals.
5. Measure retention, correction rate, classification precision, time saved, detected savings, avoided problems, and willingness to pay.
6. After retention is established, launch professional advisor seats, employer financial-wellness contracts, and provider-neutral white-label APIs.

## Defensible assets

- Financial Evidence Graph and reconciliation architecture
- Longitudinal, permissioned financial memory
- Correction-derived household rules and confidence calibration
- Provider normalization and provenance
- Document, charge, reimbursement, obligation, and payment resolution
- Explainable forecasts and proposal-only financial automation
- Cohesive product design, brand, security controls, and customer trust
- Evaluation datasets and measurable real-world outcomes

## Protection strategy

Use layered protection:

- Copyright for eligible human-authored code, specifications, writing, and visual expression.
- Trademark for Meridian and distinctive identifiers after clearance.
- Trade secrets for confidential scoring, prompts, evaluation systems, reconciliation techniques, and proprietary data.
- Patent review for concrete, novel technical mechanisms in evidence reconciliation, confidence propagation, temporal matching, correction learning, and action-safety boundaries.

The general use case, program logic, method, system, and interface layout cannot be monopolized through copyright. A provisional patent application may preserve an early U.S. filing date, but public disclosure can impair foreign rights. Obtain qualified intellectual-property counsel before publishing detailed invention mechanics.

## Commercial expansion

After consumer validation:

- Advisor edition: permissioned household cockpit priced per professional and client seat.
- Employer benefit: private employee financial-wellness product with aggregate-only employer reporting.
- White-label platform: annual platform fee plus active-user or API usage for credit unions, payroll companies, accounting platforms, and personal-finance applications.

Avoid autonomous money movement, investment recommendations, lending, insurance lead generation, and broad bank sales until the product, controls, and regulatory posture are mature.
