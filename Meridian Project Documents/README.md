# Meridian Project Documents

This folder is the single document hub for the Meridian product program.

## Product and planning

1. **Product Design Specification** - approved product architecture, experience, safety boundaries, Financial Evidence Graph, and final-priority Connected Billers design.
2. **Implementation Roadmap** - the detailed task-by-task delivery plan.
3. **Model and Token Strategy** - model routing, quality controls, and estimated build economics.
4. **Project Memory** - durable record of decisions, product principles, commercial direction, and engineering state.
5. **IP and Commercialization Brief** - protection strategy, positioning, pricing, and go-to-market sequence.

The first five documents are supplied in both editable Markdown and presentation-ready PDF formats.

## Engineering evidence

6. **Task 1 Engineering Report** - production configuration, CI, audit, Docker, browser-smoke, and review evidence.
7. **Task 2 Engineering Report** - atomic and idempotent execution implementation and concurrency-test evidence.
8. **Task 3 Engineering Report** - versioned migration, normalized read-model, freshness, provenance, pagination, and recovery evidence.
9. **Meridian Design Atlas** - interactive proposals for the four core workspaces in desktop and mobile form, plus Advisor, AI Review, Document Intelligence, and Connected Billers.
